# Capture and outbox reference

The long form of the rules in `apple/Packages/IrisCapture/AGENTS.md`, with the failures that taught them. Design: TDD §4.

- **Segment files** (Wave 18 I2): `SegmentWriter` writes each segment as a fragmented m4a (`AVAssetWriter`, `movieFragmentInterval` 0.5 s, AAC 16 kHz mono 32 kbps; the name `<memo>-<index>.m4a` and `audio/mp4` are unchanged), never an `AVAudioFile`, whose m4a has no index until it is closed and is unreadable after a kill. A force-quit leaves a file that plays up to its last whole fragment (all but about 0.5 s), and the startup sweep adopts it as the take's next segment through the existing rules; one that does not play (killed before the first fragment) is set aside, never deleted. The recorder hands a closing writer to a chain and starts the next segment at once; a segment is queued only after `finishWriting` and a read-back, and `stop()` waits for the chain before `.stopped`. A file that ends where the encoder stopped is queued with the duration it really holds and the take fails; one that does not read back is left on disk and fails the take; after either, no later segment of the take is queued (`SegmentCloser`), so the outbox never waits for a missing index, and the later files stay for the sweep. A backlog the encoder was not ready for is retried every 20 ms while the segment is open. A take killed in its first segment keeps its outbox item, hidden, until the sweep has looked for its index 0 (`OutboxStateMachine.adopt`, `settleEmptyRecoveries`): a playable file is adopted, otherwise the empty take is dropped and the file set aside; a directory that cannot be listed, a set-aside queue that cannot be read or one that names a file settles no empty take (it stays hidden until a sweep has looked). A writer that cannot be created, a buffer that cannot be built or an append the input refuses is reported by that segment's close, in order, like a failed finish; `SegmentWriter.finish` sets a `finished` flag with `markAsFinished` under its lock, so the retry task never appends to a finished input. `docs/research/2026-10-30-avaudiofile-kill.md`.

- **Capture and outbox** (Wave 15 I3, TDD §4): `IrisCapture` holds `CaptureSession` (one take: session, route, recorder, outbox) and the persistent `Outbox`, a queue of whole items
  (typed memo, chat message, voice take, thread reply) with `create` → segments → `complete`, idempotent by memo id, jittered bounded backoff, stall after 10 failures or a 4xx a retry cannot fix, "retry now" and "discard". The app reaches it through
  `OutboxService` (a lazy `CaptureStack`; `APIOutboxUploader` is the `SegmentUploader` over `APIClient`); the composer, the thread's `OutboxVoiceReplyCapture`, the Settings queue and `StartMemoIntent` (`Iris/Intents/`, compiled into the widget too) all go through it.
  A take is created on the server at its first segment, so a cancelled take leaves nothing behind. An outbox file that cannot be decoded is never emptied: Settings "Reset outbox" renames it to `outbox.quarantined-<UTC timestamp>.json` (kept, shareable from Diagnostics, its segment files untouched) and opens a fresh queue (`-IrisFakeUnreadableOutbox` shows it on fakes). A startup sweep (`Outbox.sweepOrphans(using:)` over the `SegmentFileStore` port) of the capture directory adopts segment files no record names into the take they belong to (the next index too for a finished take whose `complete` was never handed out, `OutboxMemo.completeMayHaveLanded`) or moves them to `capture/quarantine/`; nothing is deleted, and Settings shows what is kept (count, size, a share of the list; past 50 files or 200 MB a note, never an automatic delete). A file the sweep cannot move stays put, is retried at the next launch and is listed in Settings (`SweepReport.stuck`, `Outbox.strayFiles()`). A second Reset tap mid-reset joins the first. `Outbox.resume()` saves the dispatched state before it starts a transfer. A failed take whose recorder cannot be stopped is finished without the user: with its tail when the recorder queued one, else after the grace period (the user's stop leaves the finish to the pump, so a queued tail is never overtaken, even with nothing closed yet; the stop keeps its count and failure until it returns). The audio session stays active until every recorder stop has returned and the take is over: `stop()` and `cancel()` deactivate it then, behind the take gate. A take that failed on its own is not deactivated by the failure (`failTake` stops the recorder, which releases the microphone, and `TakeState.recorderReleased` says so); its session is deactivated by the user's Stop or Discard. Whether a still-active session of a failed take disturbs spoken-reply playback is unverified on the device. `complete` answered 422 `missing_segments` sends the listed segments again (`MissingSegmentsError`) instead of stalling. `OutboxMemo.completeMayHaveLanded` is always written; a queue file from before it reads "may have landed" unless its create or a segment is still open (no tail grows a completed take). `CaptureSession.stop()` / `cancel()` wait at most 10 s for `recorder.stop()` and for the recorder's last event (then the take ends with what closed and `TakeResult.failure`, or throws `recordingFailed`); a stop that timed out leaves the memo open for the tail segment and the `stopped` event (finished after a second 10 s with what closed if it never comes), so the tail is uploaded; a `recorder.stop()` that never returns refuses the next `start` until it does; `TakeController.start` is single-flight and its `cancel()` throws when the session cannot be opened. `start` / `stop` / `cancel` of a take are serialised (a gate in `CaptureSession`); `CaptureStack` never falls back to an in-memory queue (an unreadable or undecodable store refuses new items, is left untouched and is retried; an item whose write fails is refused too); Settings hides Discard on a row that is still recording, and the Live Activity follower ends the activity on a stall or discard, not on a retryable failure; `endOrphans()` (also run on a connection change) spares the takes this process is driving, and `ActivityTokenRegistrar` keeps one `PUT` per memo in flight so an older token never lands after a newer one (the gate is per memo id, so it survives `finish` + `begin` of the same memo). `JSONFileOutboxStore(segmentDirectory:)` saves each segment by file name and resolves it against the current capture directory on load (an install over the app gives the data container a new path, which stalled every segment of the first device take as "the recording file is gone"); old absolute paths are healed and a segment stalled on one is retried once. `APIOutboxUploader` answers a segment `PUT`'s 409 `bad_state` (the memo left `receiving`) by asking the server what it holds: the index is below its `segmentCount` → delivered; otherwise the file is the only copy and is set aside (`keeping`, the sweep's `quarantine/`), never deleted. A create that was on the wire at a kill is re-sent after relaunch, and a discard before that deletes the memo (the dispatch is saved before the request goes out). `LiveActivityPhase` has the client-only `recording` / `paused` / `uploading` (the wire and fixtures keep four). **A resume the system refuses while the app is not active keeps the take paused** (Issue 215, built, device-UNVERIFIED; D1-5: a Siri interruption left Iris in the background and `setActive` failed with `!int` four times, which failed the take): `AVAudioSessionController.activate()` throws `CaptureError.sessionRefused` for `!int` / `!rec` (every `CaptureError` switch treats it like `sessionFailure`), and when `AppActivity` (`UIKitAppActivity`; `nil` on the Mac = always active) says the app is not active, `CaptureSession` does not fail the take: it stays `paused` with its segments, logs `audio.interruption` `resume_deferred`, does not burn the retries, and retries the activation when the app becomes active (`appBecameActive`, behind the gate, only for a wait it recorded: a take the user paused is never resumed by a foreground transition). Stop (or Discard) while waiting finishes (drops) the take as for any paused take; the wait is bounded by `backgroundResumeLimit` (10 min), after which the take ends `failed` ("recording was not resumed in time") and sends what it has. A refusal in the foreground and every other error still fail the take (`sessionLost`); `mediaServicesReset` shares `resumeRecorder` and so the rule; the session category and `.mixWithOthers` are untouched. The take timer stands still on `failed` as on `paused` (`TakeModel.timerFrozenAt`), and the Live Activity's timer too (device-side `timerStart` / `timerFrozenAt` in `LiveActivityContentState`, like `inputName` never sent by the server). The Record control runs `OpenRecorderIntent: OpenIntent` (`authenticationPolicy = .requiresAuthentication`, compiled into app and widget): a control's plain intent runs in the extension, which cannot record, and a Lock Screen button is inactive until unlock, so the press asks for the unlock, opens the app and starts the take in the foreground; a failed start is `TakeModel.failure` and thrown. `StartMemoIntent` (`AudioRecordingIntent, LiveActivityIntent`, background start) is for Siri and Shortcuts. Built, device-UNVERIFIED (Issue 212, from the D1-3 failure: `setActive` failed with `!int` before the Live Activity was ever requested): when the app is not active, `CompositionRoot.startMemoFromShortcut` starts the take activity-first (`TakeController.start(activityFirst:)`: the memo id is minted first, `TakeLiveActivityCoordinator.requestActivity`, then `CaptureSession.start(_:memoID:)`, then `attach` of the follower); a refused activity (`TakeLiveActivity.start` returns `LiveActivityRequestResult`, logged as `liveactivity.request`) or a session or engine failure (`BackgroundStartRefusal`) ends the requested activity at once, leaves no outbox item and throws `StartMemoError.backgroundStartRefused`. `StartMemoIntent` declares `supportedModes = [.background, .foreground(.dynamic)]` and `StartMemoFallback` answers that error with `continueInForeground` and a foreground-order start (`intent.road` `background` or `foreground_fallback`); without a foreground mode the localized error is thrown. `OpenRecorderIntent` and app starts keep the session-first order. `.mixWithOthers` is not used (owner constraint). A pause keeps the engine running and drops the samples (the island's Resume runs with the app in the background, where starting an engine is refused: `!rec`). Without a push token (Personal Team) a handed-over Live Activity is ended by the app (`StaleActivityRule` / `StaleActivitySweeper` in `TakeLiveActivity.swift`: polled while the app is on screen, ended with `ActivityDismissal.soon` (the system removes it) when the app goes to the background or the hand-over happens there, swept at launch and foreground, displaced by a starting take; older than 30 min always ends). An activity remembers its server (`MemoAttributes.serverKey`, a digest of the URL): after a server-URL switch the new server's 404 does not end it (it is not asked about; only age ends it). The launch sweep asks about all activities at once, is single-flight, is repeated on foregrounding (a follower does not poll while suspended), and every end is logged with its reason. Outbox work is bound to the server it was queued for (Wave 19 I1): `OutboxItem.serverKey` / `OutboxDeletion.serverKey` are stamped from `CaptureStack.Parts.serverKey` when the item is queued, the state machine hands out nothing bound to another server (it waits, visible, and resumes when that server is configured again), `ReconfigurableAPIClient` checks `OutboxTransfer.serverKey` at the moment it picks its target (`WrongServerError` = back to pending, no attempt), and `HistoryModel` (`serverKey`) drops pages, refreshes and created memos of a server it has left; a DELETE's settle and its hide reach History in either order. `SegmentCloser` bounds `finishWriting` (30 s; a hung finish fails that close in order and leaves the file for the sweep), `cancel()` removes the capture directory's files of the memo (`SegmentFileStore.discardFiles(of:)`), and a take that failed on its own releases `captureActive` once its recorder stop returned (`TakeState.recorderReleased`). Device-only: Control press, background take, Bluetooth, Live Activity rendering.

- **Capture and outbox failures:** the outbox stalls a transfer on a refusal (`PermanentTransferError`) or after 10 non-transient failures; no network, a
  5xx, a 408 and a 429 are `TransientTransferError` and never count. `OutboxService.resume()` skips the backoff (launch, new connection settings, app
  foreground); it does not touch a stalled item. A recorder `.failed` event puts the take in phase `failed`; `CaptureSession.stop()` throws
  `CaptureError.recordingFailed` when nothing was kept. A segment is withheld from upload until a save that carries it has landed (retried on a timer, `Outbox.saveFailure()` shows in Settings). `SendOutcome.stalled` is a refusal the server will repeat: the composer keeps the text and shows the reason, and the item is not left in the queue. Live Activity calls name the memo (`TakeLiveActivity` is per memo, one follower each). A take cancelled (or an item discarded) after the server may have created its memo
  queues a `DELETE /v1/memos/:id` (`OutboxDeletion`, persisted, retried with backoff, done only on a 200 / 404 / 409; a stall stays in the queue as a "Discarded memo" row with Retry now / Stop trying and is never dropped; it waits for a `create` still on the wire; queued whenever `OutboxItem.createMayHaveLanded` (the create was handed out and not refused), also after a lost answer or a kill, never for a refused create): the take leaves the queue at once, `CreatedMemoLog.forget` makes History hide it (only once the outbox opened)
  (`HistoryModel.hide`, kept out of loads and late inserts), and a late segment or `complete` (409 `bad_state`) finds nothing queued, so it is dropped, never a stall.
