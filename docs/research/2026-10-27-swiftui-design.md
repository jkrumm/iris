## Bottom line

- **Liquid Glass in SwiftUI is `glassEffect(_:in:)` + `GlassEffectContainer` + `.buttonStyle(.glass)`**, all six platforms 26.0+, with `Glass.identity` as the documented opt-out; iOS/macOS 27 adds bar-behaviour APIs (`ToolbarOverflowMenu`, `TabRole.prominent`, `toolbarMinimizationBehavior`, vertical toolbar control) but no rename or deprecation of the glass APIs.
- **Use Apple's native glass APIs as the base; the third-party glass kits are not ready.** Among the component libraries, SwiftUI-Introspect (27.1.0, MIT, iOS/macOS 27-aware) and Vortex (1.0.4, MIT) are safe; Pow is MIT and alive but does no Liquid Glass work; DSWaveformImage (14.5.0, MIT) is the waveform pick; Inferno carries 2023-era tags despite 2026 commits.
- **IceCubesApp is the standout study target** (AGPL-3.0, 7,093★, 2.1.6 on 2026-10-13): ~13 local SPM packages + 5 extensions, `Env` DI, Swift 6 strict concurrency with MainActor-by-default, Icon Composer icon. Apple's *Landmarks* Liquid Glass sample is the canonical API walkthrough.
- **Live Activities are well documented for the timer case** (160 pt truncation limit, 4 KB state, 8 h + 4 h lifetime, `numericText(countsDown:)`, push priorities 10 vs 5); interactive buttons work through App Intents and `LiveActivityIntent`, but the `AudioRecordingIntent` stop-flow and **all** of the rich-notification-extension / text-input-action questions remain unverified this run.
- **Swift-markdown-ui is in maintenance mode and Textual is its named successor** (MIT, iOS 18+/macOS 15+, 0.5.0 on 2026-06-15, `StructuredText`). Apple's `AttributedString(markdown:)` renders inline styling only — no blocks, tables, or images — so it is not sufficient for LLM answers, and Textual's tolerance for streamed/partial markdown is unconfirmed.

## 1. Apple's Liquid Glass SwiftUI APIs

**The core modifier.** `nonisolated func glassEffect(_ glass: Glass = .regular, in shape: some Shape = DefaultGlassEffectShape()) -> some View` — "Applies the Liquid Glass effect to a view" — available on iOS 26.0+, iPadOS 26.0+, Mac Catalyst 26.0+, macOS 26.0+, tvOS 26.0+, watchOS 26.0+. Defaults are `.regular` inside `DefaultGlassEffectShape`, "a capsule". Two structural rules from Apple's article: the glass is anchored to the view's *bounds including padding*, and the modifier must come **after** other appearance modifiers because it captures content to send to the container. ([glassEffect](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/view/glasseffect(_:in:)), [Applying Liquid Glass to custom views](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/applying-liquid-glass-to-custom-views.md))

**`Glass`** is a struct (`Equatable`, `Sendable`, `SendableMetatype`) with type properties `regular`, `clear`, `identity` and instance methods `tint(Color?) -> Glass` and `interactive(Bool) -> Glass`, same six-platform 26.0+ availability. **`Glass.identity` is the documented opt-out**: "When applied, your content remains unaffected as if no glass effect was applied." ([Glass](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/glass))

**Container and morphing.** `GlassEffectContainer(spacing:)` — Apple's own sample uses `spacing: 40.0`. The `spacing` value decides when neighbouring glass shapes visually merge: a container spacing *larger* than the inner stack's spacing makes effects blend at rest, so choose it deliberately. Coordinate morphs with `glassEffectID(_:in:)` and `glassEffectTransition(_:)`, which only act during hierarchy transitions/animations. `GlassEffectTransition` offers `matchedGeometry` (default for effects inside the container's spacing) and `materialize` (for effects farther apart than the spacing); Apple says pick one style and use it consistently app-wide. `glassEffectUnion(id:namespace:)` lets separate views (e.g. dynamically created ones, or ones outside a layout container) contribute to a single unified glass shape. ([Applying Liquid Glass…](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/applying-liquid-glass-to-custom-views.md))

**Buttons.** `GlassButtonStyle` ("applies glass border artwork based on the button's context") and `GlassProminentButtonStyle` (the prominent variant) are documented, and Apple's article writes `.buttonStyle(.glass)`. Note: the literal `.glassProminent` spelling did not appear on any page retrieved — only the type name `GlassProminentButtonStyle`.

**Bars, tabs, toolbars.** The June 2025 SwiftUI wave added `glassEffect(_:in:)`, `PrimitiveButtonStyle.glass`, `ToolbarSpacer` (visual breaks between glass toolbar items), `scrollEdgeEffectStyle(_:for:)`, `backgroundExtensionEffect()`, `tabBarMinimizeBehavior(_:)`, `TabRole.search` (search field replaces the tab bar), and `TabViewBottomAccessoryPlacement`. The **June 2026** update ("build your project in Xcode 27 or later") adds `visibilityPriority(_:)`, `ToolbarOverflowMenu`, `ToolbarItemPlacement.topBarPinnedTrailing`, `toolbarMinimizationBehavior(_:for:)`, and `TabRole.prominent`. The **September 2026** update adds vertical-axis bar control: reading `toolbarVerticalEdge`, `toolbarVerticalBehavior(_:)`, `axisBehavior(_:)` with `ToolbarItemAxisBehavior` (`.horizontalOnly`, `.verticalPreferred`), and `toolbarVerticalCompressionBehavior(_:)` with `ToolbarVerticalCompressionBehavior` (e.g. `.prefersToolbarItems`, `.prefersTabBar`). No Liquid Glass deprecation or rename appears between the 2025 and 2026 lists. ([SwiftUI updates](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/updates/swiftui))

**Performance.** Apple states it plainly: "Creating too many Liquid Glass effect containers and applying too many effects to views outside of containers can degrade performance. Limit the use of Liquid Glass effects onscreen at the same time." ([Applying Liquid Glass…](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/applying-liquid-glass-to-custom-views.md))

*Not established this run:* HIG guidance (legibility, where glass is appropriate), sheet/sidebar-specific glass APIs, `.toolbarBackground` and shared-background visibility, and OS version numbers for the 27-era symbols (the updates page lists them by date, not by availability line).

## 4. Live Activities, Dynamic Island, and rich notifications

**(a) Live Activity structure.** A Live Activity is widget-extension UI: `ActivityConfiguration(for: YourAttributes.self) { context in … } dynamicIsland: { context in … }`, driven by an `ActivityAttributes` struct whose inner `ContentState` is `Codable & Hashable`. Set `NSSupportsLiveActivities = YES` in Info.plist; start with `Activity.request(attributes:content:pushType:)`, wrapping state in `ActivityContent` (optional `staleDate`, `relevanceScore`). ([Displaying live data with Live Activities](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/displaying-live-data-with-live-activities))

**(b) Dynamic Island layout.** `DynamicIsland` takes `expandedContent` (built with `@DynamicIslandExpandedContentBuilder` returning `DynamicIslandExpandedRegion`) plus `compactLeading`, `compactTrailing`, `minimal`. Region semantics: `.center` sits below the TrueDepth camera, `.leading`/`.trailing` flank it and wrap extra content below, `.bottom` spans beneath all three. Use `init(_:priority:content:)` to let a region render full width, and the `belowIfTooWide` modifier to force leading content under the camera. For reduced-width presentations — landscape iPhone, iPhone Duo, and iPhone 18 Pro with two or more active activities — fit compact content inside a **28-point circle** and branch on the `isDynamicIslandLimitedInWidth` environment value; an empty trailing compact view is hidden automatically.

**Hard limits to design around:** the system may truncate an activity whose height exceeds **160 points**; an image asset larger than the presentation size can make the activity fail to start; static + dynamic data combined **≤ 4 KB** (updates included); lifetime is **8 hours active**, then the activity leaves the Dynamic Island immediately but stays on the Lock Screen up to **4 more hours (12 h maximum)**.

**Timer without burning update budget.** The system ignores `withAnimation` and `animation(_:value:)` in Live Activities and applies its own timing; the docs direct you to request timer animations with `numericText(countsDown:)`, and to check `isLuminanceReduced` before animating on Always-On displays. For push-driven updates: an unspecified or `10` `apns-priority` is delivered immediately but **counts against the per-hour budget**, while `apns-priority: 5` does not — Apple recommends mixing the two to avoid throttling. If you need more, set `NSSupportsLiveActivitiesFrequentUpdates = YES` (user-toggleable), then read `frequentPushesEnabled` and observe `frequentPushEnablementUpdates` to adapt server cadence. Push type is `liveactivity`, topic `<bundleID>.push-type.liveactivity`. ([Live Activities push notifications](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/activitykit/starting-and-updating-live-activities-with-activitykit-push-notifications))

**Interactive buttons.** Live Activities accept SwiftUI buttons and toggles by adopting App Intents — use the `Button`/`Toggle` initializers that take an intent; note that buttons and toggles **do not act in CarPlay**. An intent conforming to `LiveActivityIntent` (`protocol LiveActivityIntent : SystemIntent`, "an intent that starts, pauses, or otherwise modifies a Live Activity when it runs") may start an activity from `perform()` while backgrounded. `AudioRecordingIntent` (`protocol AudioRecordingIntent : SystemIntent`) is documented as "An app intent that starts, stops or otherwise modifies audio recording state" and causes the system to show an app-recording indicator — but only a search snippet of that page was seen, so **a documented `AudioRecordingIntent`-based stop-recording flow is unverified**. A developer forum thread reports that triggering `AudioRecordingIntent` from Shortcuts / the Action button in the background fails with "Live Activity start failed". No dedicated entitlement appeared in the docs read beyond the Push Notifications capability. ([LiveActivityIntent](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents/liveactivityintent), [AudioRecordingIntent](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents/audiorecordingintent), [forum thread 815725](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/815725))

**(c)/(d) Rich notification content extension and text-input reply — unverified.** `UNNotificationContentExtension` metadata surfaced with availability "iOS 10.0.0 –" and no visible upper bound, which is **not** evidence of deprecation; the UIHostingController hosting pattern, any iOS 26/27 deprecation, and Apple's modern replacement could not be established. `UNTextInputNotificationAction` / `UNNotificationAction` / `UNNotificationCategory` current status and limits were not established at all. HIG Live Activities specifications (including any recording-specific guidance) were also not reached. Treat this whole sub-area as open and check `developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotificationsui/unnotificationcontentextension` and `…/usernotifications` directly.

## 5. Rendering markdown / LLM answers

**Apple's `AttributedString(markdown:)` is not enough for chat answers.** `init(markdown:options:baseURL:) throws` and variants exist, and `MarkdownParsingOptions` (iOS 15+/macOS 12+) exposes `allowsExtendedAttributes`, `appliesSourcePositionAttributes`, `failurePolicy` (with a `FailurePolicy` enum), `interpretedSyntax` (with an `InterpretedSyntax` enum) and `languageCode`. But parsing produces runs carrying `InlinePresentationIntent` and `LinkAttribute` — block constructs become `PresentationIntent` attributes that plain `Text` does not render as blocks. So `Text(AttributedString(markdown:))` is effectively inline styling only: no tables, footnotes, task lists, or images. ([Instantiating Attributed Strings with Markdown Syntax](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/instantiating-attributed-strings-with-markdown-syntax), [MarkdownParsingOptions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/attributedstring/markdownparsingoptions))

**swift-markdown-ui is in maintenance mode, not deprecated.** MIT, `archived: false`, latest release **2.4.1** (2024-10-13), description literally "Maintenance mode — new development in Textual: https://github.com/gonzalezreal/textual". The README's opening NOTE and discussion #437 (announced 2025-12-28) state it is "not being removed or archived", releases "remain available and supported", fixes and maintenance improvements are still accepted, and "New features and larger architectural work will happen in Textual". It renders GFM including images, headings, lists (incl. task lists), blockquotes, code blocks, tables and thematic breaks; min macOS 12/iOS 15/tvOS 15/watchOS 8 (tables and multi-image paragraphs need macOS 13+/iOS 16+). ([repo](https://github.com/gonzalezreal/swift-markdown-ui), [README](https://github.com/gonzalezreal/swift-markdown-ui/blob/HEAD/README.md), [discussion #437](https://github.com/gonzalezreal/swift-markdown-ui/discussions/437))

**Textual is the successor — correct names.** Repo `gonzalezreal/textual`, MIT, created 2025-12-27, latest release **0.5.0** (2026-06-15). Package name is `textual`, product is `Textual`:

```swift
.package(url: "https://github.com/gonzalezreal/textual", from: "0.1.0")
.product(name: "Textual", package: "textual")
```

Platforms are `.macOS(.v15), .iOS(.v18), .tvOS(.v18), .watchOS(.v11), .visionOS(.v2)` at swift-tools 6.0, depending on `swift-concurrency-extras` 1.3.1, `swift-snapshot-testing` 1.18.7 and `gonzalezreal/swiftui-math` 0.1.0, and bundling Prism grammars as a resource (`Internal/Highlighter/Prism`) — that is the syntax-highlighting engine. (The npm package named "textual" is an unrelated Node project.)

It describes itself as "the spiritual successor to MarkdownUI" and ships two entry points: **`InlineText`** (a drop-in replacement for `Text` with attachment support) and **`StructuredText`** (block-based documents). Markdown comes via Foundation's `AttributedString` parser, with a pluggable `MarkupParser` protocol for other formats. Features: inline attachments (images, custom emoji via `syntaxExtensions: [.emoji(…)]`, animated GIF/APNG/WebP), math as inline/block attachments via `syntaxExtensions: [.math]`, "Syntax highlighting with customizable themes", built-in `.default` and `.gitHub` presets via `.textual.structuredTextStyle(_:)`, per-block overrides (`headingStyle`, `codeBlockStyle`), the full `StructuredText.Style` protocol for custom themes, `.fontScaled()` for relative fonts, and text selection via `.textual.textSelection(_:)`. ([Package.swift](https://github.com/gonzalezreal/textual/blob/HEAD/Package.swift), [README](https://github.com/gonzalezreal/textual/blob/HEAD/README.md), [0.5.0 release](https://github.com/gonzalezreal/textual/releases/tag/0.5.0))

**Streaming is unconfirmed.** Nothing in Textual's README, Package.swift or release notes claims tolerance for partial markdown; the 0.5.0 notes are bug fixes plus new Prism language definitions. Test before building a streamed-answer UX on it.

**Highlighters, if you roll your own.** Runestone (`simonbs/Runestone`, MIT, 0.5.2 on 2026-03-25, active) is the live one, but it is a tree-sitter-based *editor* with highlighting, not a lightweight code-block renderer. Splash is dormant (MIT, last push 2024-05-27, latest release 0.16.0 from 2021). HighlightSwift is MIT, v1.1.0 (2024-06-25), last push 2024-11-27. NSHipster's SwiftSyntaxHighlighter was archived in 2021 (search snippet only). ([Runestone](https://github.com/simonbs/Runestone), [Splash](https://github.com/JohnSundell/Splash), [HighlightSwift](https://github.com/appstefan/HighlightSwift), [SwiftSyntaxHighlighter](https://github.com/NSHipster/SwiftSyntaxHighlighter))

**Architecture guidance (engineering judgement, not from a source).** Keep markdown in a model; split it into stable completed blocks plus one trailing growing block; render completed blocks once and re-parse only the tail; give each code block its own view keyed by language so highlighting runs once per block rather than per token. Since Textual's `StructuredText` already yields block-level views, cache per-block parsed content and use `swiftui-math` for LaTeX.
