Pictures: iPhone 17 / iOS 27.0 (`w21/`, downscaled to 1000 px on the long side) and the Mac main window and popover (`IrisMac -IrisFakeData
-IrisMacSnapshot`). The iOS shots come from a throwaway UI-test walk (not committed) on the fake stack at the pinned clock; the Mac ones from the
app's own snapshot run (the display must be awake: an asleep display makes every chrome window a blank PNG).

## Record row (first)

Three visible buttons, no hidden gesture: a large filled Hermes button (`record.start`), a smaller Transcribe button (`record.transcribe`) and a
smaller Text button (`record.text`, opens the composer). The Hermes glyph is `sparkles`: the SF Symbols of the SDK have no robot glyph (owner call).
In the minimized tab bar (`inline` placement) the two small buttons drop their titles. Crops of the accessory:
